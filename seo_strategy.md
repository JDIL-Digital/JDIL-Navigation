# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Global crawl and bot-governance files (`robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`) and the shared HTML shell.
- Public unauthenticated utility routes:
  - `/login`
  - `/admin-login`
  - `/register`
  - `/forgot-password`
  - `/reset-password`
- Any source-visible metadata, social tags, canonical tags, and indexing directives affecting those public routes.

## Out of scope
- Authenticated application routes and internal workflow pages:
  - `/`
  - `/purchase`
  - `/logistics`
  - `/rig`
  - `/admin`
  - `/repair`
  - `/return`
  - `/replacement`
  - `/reverse-logistics`
- Deep dashboard UX, internal analytics screens, and non-public operational content.

## Target audience
- Internal JDIL employees and administrators using the material tracking system.

## Primary keywords
- None identified. This application appears to be an internal-use system rather than a search-acquisition surface.

## Dismissed categories
- (None yet)

## Notes
- Because this is an internal-use app with no marketing or editorial surface, the highest-value SEO work is controlling how search, social, and AI crawlers treat public auth/utility routes.
- If the business later adds public marketing pages, re-scope future scans to include route-level title, description, Open Graph, structured data, and internal linking audits for those pages.
